The Gulf — Lead story

IRAN STRIKES U.S. BASES IN KUWAIT AND BAHRAIN

Tehran fires drones and missiles at American bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, a day after U.S. jets hit a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island. Gulf air defences say the attacks were intercepted — the war’s ninth month turns wider.

Sources: Reuters, Just Security
Asia — Diplomacy

XI AND PUTIN MEET IN SHENZHEN

China’s Xi and Russia’s Putin open talks a day before their first three-way summit with Donald Trump, who arrives in the city. Ukraine, Iran and nuclear arms are on the agenda.

United States — Politics

PELTOLA WINS ALASKA, DEMOCRATS TAKE 56–44

Democrat Mary Peltola unseats Sen. Dan Sullivan in the last Senate race called, giving Democrats a 56–44 majority after flipping nine Republican seats.

Pacific — Weather

CYCLONE XAVIER, STRONGEST NOVEMBER STORM ON RECORD

The Category 5 cyclone bears down on the Northern Cook Islands at 220 km/h, with gale warnings for Pukapuka and Nassau.

Health — Africa

EBOLA EMERGENCY HOLDS AS CASES PASS 11,000

WHO keeps its highest alert as Congo’s outbreak passes 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths, still adding 400–500 cases a week.

Science

VOYAGER 1: ONE LIGHT-DAY FROM HOME

NASA’s 1977 probe reaches one light-day from Earth — 16 billion miles — at 5:16 a.m. EST. A signal now takes a full day to reach it.
